# Launch plan

WSL containers (WSLC) became generally available on 29 September 2026. WSLC
AI Agent went public the same night: repository, CI green, release 1.0.0
with its installers. The launch on X is the morning after.

## When

**30 September 2026, 07:00 in Colombia (12:00 UTC)**: 08:00 on the US East
Coast, 14:00 in Spain. Europe fully awake, the US starting its day, and the
WSLC announcement still less than a day old and still being read. The US
West Coast wakes three hours later: a repost from the other account around
10:00 in Colombia catches it.

## Where

1. **The X article** ([x-article.md](x-article.md)), from the account with
   the most reach (Berpiztu or the personal one).
2. **The post quoting Craig Loewen's announcement**
   ([x-posts.md](x-posts.md), "Main post"), with the MCP video attached and
   links to the repository and the article. Right after the article, so the
   post can link to it.
3. **The thread**: the second post as a reply to the first.
4. **Repost** from the other account.

Announcements worth quoting, by relevance:

| Post | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| https://x.com/craigaloewen/status/2104985841034506577 | Craig Loewen, WSL product manager | **The one.** He is WSLC's author, is notified by a quote and answers people; his audience is exactly ours |
| https://x.com/windowsblog/status/2104985851558064454 | Windows Blogs (459k) | The biggest reach; institutional, does not answer |
| https://x.com/windowsdev/status/2104991690733896086 | Windows Developer (511k) | Developers, with `wsl --update` |

## The first hours

- Answer every reply, fast and warmly. The first two hours decide how far a
  post travels.
- Likely questions and their answers: [x-posts.md](x-posts.md), "Answers".
- Watch the repository: stars, issues, the release's downloads
  (Insights → Traffic shows visits and where they came from).
- A question that comes twice is a missing line in the README: add it.

## Later

- A Spanish version for the Spanish-speaking audience.
- LinkedIn: the article, adapted.
- Communities: r/bashonubuntuonwindows, r/selfhosted, the MCP and Claude
  communities, Hacker News ("Show HN").
- If Craig or the WSL team answers, thank them, and ask whether it could
  appear in the WSL documentation or community projects.
